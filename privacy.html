Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Garden Games Inc. (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you play Strange Game or visit strange.game.

Information We Collect

  • Gameplay and Device Data: We collect basic device information (such as model and operating system) and gameplay data (like progress, preferences, and session activity) to improve performance and player experience.
  • Analytics and Crash Reports: We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to help us understand app performance and diagnose crashes. This data is anonymous and does not include personally identifiable information.
  • User-Generated Content: When you use AI image generation features in the game, your text prompts and generated images are sent to third-party AI model providers (such as FAL.ai or similar services) to create and deliver the requested images. These prompts and images are then stored by Garden Games Inc. as part of your gameplay history and may be displayed to other players within the game (for example, in galleries, leaderboards, or community voting features). You may delete your generated content at any time by contacting us at gerald@garden.games.

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate and improve Strange Game;
  • Fix crashes and technical issues;
  • Maintain gameplay integrity and moderate content;
  • Analyze gameplay trends and player engagement (in aggregate only).

Third-Party Services

Our app and website may use the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • fal.ai Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics)
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • AWS Privacy Policy

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain minimal gameplay and analytics data only as long as necessary to operate the game. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at gerald@garden.games.

Children’s Privacy

Strange Game is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect or store personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact us for deletion.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most recent version will always be available at strange.game/privacy.
